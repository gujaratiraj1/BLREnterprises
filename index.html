About BLR Enterprises

Pioneering Engineering & Logistics in Bengaluru

Established in Bengaluru, Karnataka, BLR Enterprises is a registered Micro Enterprise managed under the proprietorship of Rajeswari Gujarati. We have built our reputation on a commitment to core engineering excellence and seamless supply chain dispatch.

Our operational framework leverages a dual-service capability model, offering robust design-build solutions for civil construction and highly organized road transport networks. We bridge the gap between design theory and physical delivery.
